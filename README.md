Midnight Ramen — a late-night ramen bar cooking game. Tickets come in on the order board: simmer a broth until it is ready, drop noodles in the boiler and pull them in the green zone for perfect noodles, grab two toppings onto your tray, build the bowl at the assembly counter, and serve it at the window before the customer walks out. A 120-second shift, combo scoring, a rush hour in the last minute, and perfect noodles pay +30.

## How it plays

- **Broth pots** (left wall): Shoyu, Miso and Tonkotsu simmer on their own timers. Pick up a broth only when its pot reads READY.
- **Noodle boiler**: press E to drop noodles, press E again to pull — in the green window for perfect noodles, too early is undercooked, too late is soggy.
- **Topping rail** (bottom): Ajitama Egg, Chashu, Nori, Narutomaki, Corn and Scallion. Your tray holds up to 4 things.
- **Assembly counter**: press E to deposit your tray into the bowl. A bowl that matches a ticket lights up READY TO SERVE.
- **Serving window**: press E to serve the finished bowl. Wrong bowls go in the trash.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open the game in a desktop browser (or open `game/index.html` directly). Controls: WASD / arrows to move, E to interact, Space to start, P to pause, R to restart, M to mute. Mouse: click to walk, click a station to walk to it and use it.
